## 2026-10-06 correction

The initial files described here were PNGs. E2CC metadata and timelines loaded, but the live Kit log showed that release 1.1.0 rejects PNG timestamp sequences and only invokes its dynamic-texture loader for JPEG suffixes. The invalid texture appeared as triangular cutoffs, black sectors, and broad flat color bands.

The installed E2CC weather-image operation emits single-channel grayscale JPEGs,
so the exporter and all channel outputs were regenerated as grayscale,
baseline, quality-99 JPEGs. Offline mode, dimensions, paths, and checksums pass.
Decoded-frame comparison with the normalized HDF5 source has a maximum error of
2 on the 0–255 scale and more than 99.996% of pixels within one level.

Interim live tests appeared to become posterized and partly black, leading to
investigation of JPEG variants, Dynamic Texture, Kit, the shader cache, and the
Blackwell GPU. The same degradation later affected the built-in Base Satellite
and ordinary UI text and could recover while E2CC was untouched. The actual
cause was RealVNC's automatic adaptive picture quality. Setting RealVNC
**Picture quality** to **High** and **PreferredEncoding** to **ZRLE** eliminated
the artifact.

## Live validation completed

Both regenerated metadata files load successfully in E2CC and run through all
seven timestamps without the previous apparent collapse. `q850` was validated
in the same session. The temporary-reference layers are model lead-0 output, not
ERA5 ground truth.
